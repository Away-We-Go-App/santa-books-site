Santa Books Support

Need help with your account, photos or Christmas books? Email sebastian@thesantabooks.com.

Tell us what you were trying to do, what happened, and your app version and iPhone model. You can find the version in Santa Books Settings. Remove private details from screenshots before sending them, and don’t send passwords or sign-in codes.

Account deletion

Open Santa Books Settings → Help & support → Delete account and follow the confirmation steps. Your original photos in iPhone Photos are unaffected. If the app reports an error, follow its retry message or contact support with the error shown. Our Privacy Policy explains deletion and retained order information.
