Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Santa Books uses information to run the app, support your account, and help you preserve Christmas memories in books.

Information we use

This includes account and sign-in information, photos you import or allow the app to gather for a Christmas year, captions and book text, layouts, photo choices, and related metadata such as capture dates. Your year’s photo pool can sync to your Santa account even when some photos have not been placed on book pages. Book edits do not change or delete your original photos in iPhone Photos.

We use this information to save and restore your years, keep devices in sync, secure accounts, and respond to support requests. If payment and printing features are available and you choose to use them, we also process the order, payment references, recipient and shipping details, and print files needed for those services.

Service providers and notifications

Supabase provides account authentication and cloud book/photo storage. Apple provides Sign in with Apple and notification delivery. OneSignal supports push notifications you enable. The website is hosted on Vercel; Google Workspace handles support email, and Resend supports email delivery. When ordering is available, Stripe processes payments and Prodigi handles printing and fulfillment.

Enabling push associates your Santa account identifier and device notification token with OneSignal. Its SDK also processes device/app, network, session and notification-interaction information. The app does not attach photos or book content to that notification subscription. Turning on the Christmas book reminder also enables push; turning push off also turns the reminder off.

Santa’s shared product analytics transport is disabled, and this website has no analytics script or contact form. Hosting, authentication and other service providers may still process operational information, such as network requests and logs. Santa Books and Away We Go have separate app accounts and cloud libraries. The printing supplier account is shared for administration; that does not combine your app accounts.

Your choices and account deletion

You can manage Photos access and notification permission in iPhone Settings. Limited Photos access is supported. Signing out keeps account-local work on the device; it is not account deletion.

To delete your account, open Santa Books Settings → Help & support → Delete account. A completed deletion removes sign-in and your personal cloud library, removes your Santa notification identity, and clears that account’s local books and retained copies on the current iPhone. Copies already downloaded elsewhere are not remotely recalled. If deletion reports a failure, some steps may already have completed; follow the retry message or contact us.

Accepted print orders can finish after account deletion. Payments without an accepted order are refunded. We retain the order, contact and payment snapshots, print files containing the printed photos and text, order-email recipients, and recovery information needed to finish and resolve accepted orders. We purge retained fulfillment files and contact details 30 days after the latest required completed account deletion, delivery, payment settlement or final recovery resolution. Unresolved payment, replacement or fulfillment issues can delay when that period starts.

This does not erase every record at every provider within 30 days. Minimal deletion/payment references and operational audit records remain for late-event replay, payment/refund recovery and accountability. Operational logs may also retain identifiers. These residual records do not have a fixed expiry in the current cleanup process; we retain them for these necessary purposes, subject to applicable requirements. Provider financial records, backups, logs and support correspondence have separate retention. Already-issued print-file links can remain usable until their existing expiry, up to 30 minutes; deletion prevents the former account from creating new links.
